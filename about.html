The Vulnerability

WHY THIS
MATTERS
BEYOND ME

The reason this site exists is not to relitigate my case. My case is the proof of concept. The reason this site exists is that the infrastructure used against me is available to anyone with PACER credentials — and the people receiving the documents it produces have no way to verify them.

There is no registry. No hotline. No QR code. No cryptographic signature. The "electronic signature" on a federal court filing is the text /s/Name typed into a Word document. That is the entire security model for the system that issues orders determining whether you go to prison, lose your children, forfeit your assets, or comply with a surveillance demand you are legally prohibited from disclosing.

National Security Letters. FISA warrants. Orders of commitment. Judgments of conviction. All of them rest on the same foundation: a login and a typed slash-s. A personal check from a party store has more authentication infrastructure than a federal warrant.

The fix is not complicated. A federal authentication clearinghouse. One query. One binary response: YES, this order exists and authorizes exactly what it claims — or NO, it does not. The entire architecture of document-based coercion collapses against a system that can say NO.

That system does not exist. Building it is the point of this site.
